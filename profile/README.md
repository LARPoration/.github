# LARPoration 🚧

### Intern as a Service. Experience you can point to.

“Entry-level position. Three years of experience required.”

We thought that needed a workaround.

LARPoration gives people who lack professional experience a real organisation to build, operate and improve—so they leave with verifiable work, genuine responsibility and evidence they can take into their next opportunity.

Our first project is LARPoration itself.

## What we’re building

A corpration. Working processes. A public identity. An organisation worth contributing to.

The construction signs are intentional. There is real work behind them.

## Own Your Output

- **Real work, not simulations.** Solve problems the organisation actually has.
- **Evidence over titles.** Show what you made, changed, shipped or improved.
- **Credit that lasts.** Our website is built to connect work to contributors and preserve earlier releases as it evolves.
- **Leave it understandable.** Documentation and handovers are part of the contribution.

## Intern as a Service™

Like Infrastructure as a Service, except the infrastructure is still figuring out its career.

The acronym is a joke. The work and credit aren’t.
